Account Deletion Request

Your privacy matters to us. UniKit allows registered users to request permanent deletion of their UniKit account and associated personal information.

If you no longer wish to use UniKit, you can submit an account deletion request by following the steps described on this page.

How to Request Account Deletion

You do not need to access the app to submit a deletion request. You can request deletion directly by email.

  1. Send an email to our official support address.
  2. Use the subject: UniKit Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address associated with your UniKit account.
  4. We may contact you to verify that you own the account before processing the request.
  5. Once verified, we will process your account and associated data deletion request.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

Personal data and records linked to your account will be deleted from the services controlled by UniKit, including Firebase Authentication, Firestore and applicable Supabase storage or records.

Public community content that has already been approved and published may be retained where it is no longer associated with your personal account. Personal identifiers and account-linked submission records will be removed where applicable.

Information stored only on your device, such as locally saved documents or app preferences, may need to be removed separately by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling UniKit.

  • Account profile information, such as your name and account email address.
  • DUET email verification information associated with your account.
  • Account-linked information stored in our Firebase and Firestore services.
  • Account-associated club, academic calendar, prayer-time or other submitted requests.
  • Account-associated notification records and registered push notification tokens.
  • Uploaded files and images associated with your account that are stored in UniKit's Supabase storage services.
  • Other personal information associated with your UniKit account.

Data that is not associated with your account, such as information stored only on your device, may need to be removed separately by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Data That May Not Be Deleted

Account deletion does not delete your Google account. It only applies to your UniKit account and the associated data controlled by UniKit.

Advertising-related information independently collected or processed by Google AdMob is handled according to Google's applicable privacy and data policies. Deleting your UniKit account does not automatically delete information held independently by Google.

Information may be retained only where necessary for legitimate legal, security or fraud-prevention purposes. Any retained information will be limited to what is necessary and kept only for the required period.

Data Retention and Processing Time

Verified account deletion requests will be processed within 30 days after verification and approval.

Where limited retention is legally necessary, the relevant information will be retained only for the period required for that purpose.

Once the deletion process is completed, the deleted account and associated data cannot normally be recovered.

Guest Users

Guest users do not have a registered UniKit account. Guest-session information may be stored locally on the device.

Guest users can clear local app data or uninstall UniKit to remove locally stored guest-session data.
