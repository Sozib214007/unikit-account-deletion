Account & Data Deletion

Account Deletion Request

We respect your privacy and your right to control your personal information. If you no longer wish to use UniKit, you can request permanent deletion of your account and associated data.

How to Delete Your Account

To request account deletion, follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to our support address.
  2. Use the subject: UniKit Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address linked to your UniKit account.
  4. We may contact you to verify account ownership.
  5. After verification, we will process your deletion request.

Send your deletion request here:

Request Account Deletion

What Data Will Be Deleted?

Once your request is approved, we will delete the following data associated with your account, where stored:

  • Account information, including name and email address.
  • Saved academic information associated with your account.
  • Tasks, class routines and other account-linked data.
  • Other personal information stored by UniKit.

Data Retention

Account-associated personal data will be deleted within 30 days after your request has been verified and approved.

We do not retain deleted personal data unless retention is required by applicable law. Any legally required data will be retained only for the required period.
