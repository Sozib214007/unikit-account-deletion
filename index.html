Account Deletion Request

Your privacy matters to us. UniKit gives you control over your personal information. If you no longer wish to use UniKit, you can request the permanent deletion of your account and associated personal data.

How to Delete Your Account

To request deletion of your UniKit account, please follow these simple steps:

  1. Send an email to our official support address.
  2. Use the subject: UniKit Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address associated with your UniKit account.
  4. We may contact you to verify ownership of the account.
  5. After verification, we will process your account deletion request.

Request Account Deletion

What Data Will Be Deleted?

After your deletion request is approved, the following data associated with your account will be deleted, where collected or stored:

  • Account information, including name and email address.
  • Saved academic information associated with your account.
  • Tasks, class routines and other account-linked data.
  • Other personal information stored by UniKit.

Data Retention Policy

Account-associated personal data will be deleted within 30 days after your request has been verified and approved.

We do not retain deleted personal data unless retention is required by applicable law. Any legally required data will be retained only for the period required by law.
